## Review areas

### 1. UX & Information Architecture
- Is the navigation clear and discoverable?
- Do users understand who Temi is and what they do?
- Is the path from landing → work → contact logical?
- Are calls to action clear?

### 2. Visual Design & UI
- Does the visual execution feel premium and authored (not templated)?
- Typography: hierarchy, rhythm, readability — does it carry the identity?
- Layout: spacing, alignment, composition — does it feel composed, not filled?
- Colour: is the palette used consistently and effectively?
- Imagery: do the travel photos and case study images feel art-directed?

### 3. Responsive Behaviour
- Does the layout adapt intentionally across mobile → tablet → desktop?
- Typography: does it reflow well at all widths?
- Navigation: is it usable on small screens?
- Images: are crops and sizes appropriate at each breakpoint?

### 4. Interaction & Motion
- Do SectionReveal animations feel purposeful and well-paced?
- Hover states: do links and interactive elements feel polished?
- Are interactions precise and responsive?
- Does motion respect reduced-motion preferences?

### 5. Content & Writing
- Is the writing specific, concise, and credible (not generic designer language)?
- Case studies: do they explain context, decisions, trade-offs, and outcomes?
- About page: does it communicate personality and professional depth?
- Contact page: is the tone appropriate and the CTA clear?

### 6. Accessibility
- Semantic HTML structure?
- Heading hierarchy?
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus states?
- Alt text on images?
- Colour contrast?
- Touch targets on mobile?

### 7. Performance (basic)
- Is the page weight reasonable for a static site?
- Are images appropriately sized?
- Any obvious render-blocking issues?

### 8. Edge Cases
- 404 page behaviour
- Missing images or broken links
- Social links open in new tabs correctly
- Email link opens mail client
- Sitemap.xml and robots.txt accessible
- Case study slugs resolve correctly
